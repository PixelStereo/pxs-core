## Do I need the commercial licence?

- **Using pxs-core in an open-source project that is itself AGPL-compatible**, or only *internally*: no, the AGPL is enough.
- **Linking it into a product you ship without its source**, or distributing through a store whose terms add restrictions the
  AGPL forbids: yes.
- **Pixel Stereo's own applications** are published by the copyright holder, who is not bound by the licence of their own code.

## How to ask

Open an issue on [PixelStereo/pxs-core](https://github.com/PixelStereo/pxs-core/issues) or contact the maintainer through
the [PixelStereo](https://github.com/PixelStereo) GitHub profile. Nothing in this repository grants a commercial licence
until a written agreement is signed.

## Keeping it possible: contributions

The copyright holder can license the code commercially only for what the holder owns or has the right to relicense.
Therefore contributions are accepted only under the terms in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md), which grant that right.
Third-party code that is not owned by the holder must carry its own licence, be compatible with both options above
(permissive: MIT, BSD, Boost, Apache-2.0), and be listed in `LICENSES/` so that `reuse lint` passes.
